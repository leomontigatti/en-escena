# Access

Rules for public registration, users and sessions.

- Public registration is one for an academy and for an `individual` (`Particular`, ADR-0019). It starts with email plus password and asks the access auth provider (Better Auth, ADR-0013) to send the confirmation link.
- The access auth provider owns public registration email confirmation and the confirmed academy identity session created from that email link. The confirmation URL keeps the legacy `token_hash` plus `type=signup` shape so links already emailed keep working; the current provider accepts it and it must not be "corrected".
- After the email is confirmed, the first onboarding step (`/registro/cuenta`) asks for the kind of account, `Academia` or `Particular`; each opens its own form. The academy onboarding form uses the confirmed session identity, keeps the confirmed email fixed, and collects academy data.
- The individual onboarding form collects first name, last name, document type and number (stored normalized through the identity-document owner, so dots and spaces are accepted) and phone, and creates in one transaction the user with the `individual` permission, its owner `academy` row of kind `individual` (full name as `name` and `contactName`, its phone, no city or province) and its `individual` row, or nothing. One email is one user: an email that already has a user is refused with a message saying so, and the registration and choice screens say it up front.
- If email already belongs to an existing user, public response must not reveal it.
- `Identidad confirmada pendiente de academia` means the access auth provider already confirmed the academy access identity, but academy onboarding has not created the `Academia` yet.
- If that confirmed identity returns from login or direct navigation, the app must let it resume academy onboarding instead of forcing a new registration.
- There is no automatic cleanup under this first maintenance policy; the incomplete state stays resumable and maintenance must list confirmed academy `Usuario` records with no `Academia` for a later operational decision.
- Completing academy onboarding creates the academy, keeps the confirmed academy user, authenticates the academy, and does not require admin approval.
- A `Usuario` has one main permission: `academy`, `admin`, `auditor`, `judge` or `individual` (`Particular`).
- Academy and individual users sign in with a verified email and password; each lands on its own home (`/portal`, `/particular`).
- An individual works in an area of its own, `/particular`, with its own layout and menu (`Mis datos`, `Mis coreografías`). Its guard (`requireIndividualUser`) admits the `individual` permission alone; the portal, the `Panel de administración` and the `Panel de juzgamiento` refuse it.
- An individual's link to the academy rows that carry its document (ADR-0019) is confirmed by administration from `Particulares`, after comparing the document photo with the account's name and document; the auditor reads the list and the detail and cannot confirm or revoke. The link state is derived, never stored: `Sin vincular` without a photo, `Pendiente` with one until administration confirms, `Vinculado` while the confirmation was given for the individual's current document number. Changing the document takes it back to `Pendiente` with no write of its own; `Revocar vínculo` clears the confirmation. Which `dancer` and `professor` rows the link reaches, in any academy and whatever their `Estado de alta`, is worked out on every read (`app/lib/individuals/link.server.ts`), and only while the link is `Vinculado`; `Mis coreografías` leaves the archived ones out. A confirmation names the number and the photo administration compared, and is refused when either changed meanwhile.
- Internal users sign in with a `Nombre de usuario interno` and password.
- `Nombre de usuario interno` is unique ignoring case, normalized to lowercase, 3 to 32 characters, and only accepts lowercase letters, numbers, dot, hyphen and underscore. It cannot contain spaces, accents or email-like values. `acceso` and `dmarc` are refused: they are real routed addresses on `enescena.com.ar` (`docs/operations/dns-and-email.md`) and `acceso@` is the outbound sender.
- Internal users have no email in the application: it is asked for nowhere, shown nowhere and nothing is ever sent to one. The access auth provider keys the password on a unique `user.email`, so an internal user gets a made-up credential email, `<username>@enescena.com.ar`, set once at creation from the username, which cannot be edited; an update never rewrites it. `/ingresar` resolves the typed username to that address, so nobody types it or sees it.
- Where an internal user's identity is shown — the `Panel de administración` sidebar footer and the `Juzgamiento` header — the app shows their name, their `Permiso principal` and their `Nombre de usuario interno`. The users list finds an internal user by name or username, and an academy or an individual by name or email; an individual shows with the `Particular` permission label.
- A `Usuario` auditor is read-only and cannot create, edit, publish, unpublish, cancel, correct or annul.
- The permissions, their labels and what each may do live in one module, `app/lib/auth/user-role.shared.ts`: guards, loaders and views ask its predicates (`canReadAdminPanel`, `canWriteInAdminPanel`, …) rather than comparing roles, and the request guards in `internal-access.server.ts` are built on them. A view that renders a control changing data reads the loader's `canWrite`.
- The auditor works inside the `Panel de administración`: its layout admits administration readers (`requireAdminPanelReader`), while every loader not reviewed for the auditor admits writers alone (`requireAdminPanelWriter`) and answers them 403. Their navigation and home cards are a closed list of the reviewed sections (`Academias`, `Bailarines`, `Profesores`, `Coreografías`, `Pagos`, `Seminarios`, `Particulares`), not every loader that happens to admit them; a section joins when its view has been checked to render no control that changes data. `Seminarios` is the list alone: a seminar's detail, where inscriptions are removed, stays the administrator's, so the auditor's list does not link to it. There is no separate auditor panel.
- Session inactivity limit is 8 hours for all permissions; logout affects only current session.
- Admins create internal users directly with their password, typed once on the form and shared with the person out of band; there is no first-login password change.
- Academy and individual users recover access by email through the access auth provider and define the new password on `Cambio de contraseña`.
- Internal password recovery is an administrative reset: an admin types the new password, the user's open sessions close, and the user signs in with it. `/cambiar-contrasena` serves academy and individual recovery only.
- Admins can create, edit, suspend, reactivate, reset passwords and change permissions for internal users; auditors can view users read-only.
- A user's `Estado` is `Activo` or `Suspendido` and is read the same way for every permission: a suspended user cannot sign in, keep a session or use `Recuperación de acceso`, and the users list and detail show them as `Suspendido`. The users list shows active users unless the `Suspendido` filter is chosen. Only internal users can be suspended from the application, so an academy row holding suspension was set outside it and has no reactivation in the panel.
- No one can act as another user: Better Auth's admin plugin refuses impersonation (`POST /api/auth/admin/impersonate-user`) for every permission, `admin` included, because nothing in the app would record that a session was opened by someone else. The `impersonated_by` column exists only because the plugin's schema requires it.
- An administrator's main permission cannot be changed from the application: the edit form locks it and the server refuses any change away from `admin` on an account that already holds it. Promoting another internal user to `admin` is allowed; demoting one is a database-only operation.
- Creating internal users, changing permissions, suspending or reactivating users and administrative password resets leave no administrative audit trail: there is no record of who changed what. Raw passwords and password hashes are never persisted outside the credential store.
- Internal users use the app-owned credential store and the same 8-hour session policy as academy users.
- Better Auth owns production academy credentials, public registration email confirmation, academy password recovery and academy sessions; app code owns academy onboarding, direct internal-user creation (ADR-0003) and the local test harness. Pre-cutover `sb-*` cookies are only expired by a migration shim (`app/lib/auth/legacy-session-cookies.server.ts`), not read by any provider.

## Permission Matrix

This matrix describes domain authority, not component visibility. Server guards
and actions must enforce it even when UI controls are hidden.

| Permission  | Main scope                                            | Can mutate                                                                                                                       | Cannot mutate                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| academy     | Their own data in the `Portal de academias`           | Their own academy profile, professors, dancers and choreographies, within the rules of the `Evento activo`                       | `Panel de administración`, internal users, publications, administrative corrections, other academies' data |
| admin       | Operating the `Panel de administración`               | Events, `Bases del evento`, internal users, administrative corrections, publications and the allowed actions on operational data | Technical credentials outside the defined flows                                                            |
| auditor     | Read-only over administration and audit               | Nothing in business flows                                                                                                        | Create, edit, publish, unpublish, cancel, correct, annul, suspend or reactivate                            |
| juzgamiento | The evaluation assigned in the `Panel de juzgamiento` | Scores and judge feedback (`Devolución`) within the assigned evaluation flow                                                     | General administration, `Bases del evento`, users, financial data and other areas' data                    |
| individual  | Their own personal data in their own area             | Their own personal data and document                                                                                             | The portal, the `Panel de administración`, other users' data                                               |
